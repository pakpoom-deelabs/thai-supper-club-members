Our Philosophy

Thai cuisine is not
a performance.
It is a conversation.

SALA was founded on a single belief: that the most profound meals happen when the table is small, the cook is present, and the recipes are honest. We seat eight. No more, ever.

Each evening is built around a single region of Thailand — its markets, its seasons, its forgotten traditions. Guests do not choose from a menu. They receive a meal. The distinction is everything.

"Real Thai cooking disappears when you scale it. We chose not to scale."

    The Four Regions Each supper anchors to one culinary tradition — Northern Lanna, Southern Coastal, Northeastern Isaan, or Central Royal cuisine.

Membership

Eight seats.
Earned through
curiosity.

Sala does not advertise. Membership is extended by existing members, or by written application. We look for genuine interest in Thai culture and food — not credentials or status.

  1. 01

    Write to us

    Send a brief letter explaining what Thai food means to you. No format requirements. We read everything personally.

  2. 02

    Share a meal

    Prospective members are invited to a tasting evening — hosted by current members, not the kitchen. Consider it a conversation over food.

  3. 03

    Receive your seat

    Membership grants six evenings per year, priority booking for seasonal events, and access to our private pantry of imported Thai ingredients.
