Our Philosophy

Anantara began as an obsession: could a single table, set in a quiet Bangkok townhouse, deliver the full depth of Thai culinary heritage — not as museum piece, but as living art?

Every Friday and Saturday evening, eight members sit together at one long table. The kitchen is open. The chef narrates each course. There are no menus — only the market that morning, the seasons of the land, and the generations of technique behind every hand that touches your plate.

Membership is by invitation only, renewed annually. We keep the circle intentionally small because intimacy cannot be scaled.

The Menu

Seven courses,
written in Thai.

No menu card is placed at your seat. What arrives is dictated by the week's market, the chef's memory, and the seasons of the Central Plains. What follows is a glimpse into the language of a typical evening.

Course 01 · Amuse
Miang Kham
เมี่ยงคำ
Wild betel leaf, toasted coconut, dried shrimp, ginger, lime, and a tamarind glaze that holds twelve memories in a single bite.
Course 02 · Soup
Tom Kha Krai
ต้มข่าไก่
Galangal-steeped coconut broth with young lemongrass, kaffir lime, and heritage chicken from Chiang Rai. Six-hour stock. Zero shortcuts.
Course 03 · Cold
Yam Mamuang
ยำมะม่วง
Green mango salad with crispy shallots, chilli, fish sauce caramel, and a snow of smoked dried fish from the Gulf.
Course 04 · Curry
Gaeng Kiao Wan
แกงเขียวหวาน
Slow-simmered green curry with hand-pounded paste, eggplant blossoms, and kra pao leaves harvested from the kitchen garden at dawn.
Course 05 · Sea
Pla Neung Manao
ปลานึ่งมะนาว
Whole barramundi, steamed over banana leaf with lime, garlic, and coriander root. Served with a broth so clear it appears invisible.
Course 06 · Earth
Khao Pad Sapparod
ข้าวผัดสับปะรด
Jasmine rice, fried in aged lard, with fresh pineapple, cashew, and turmeric-stained egg. A course that insists on its own unpretentiousness.
Course 07 · Sweet
Khao Niao Mamuang
ข้าวเหนียวมะม่วง
Glutinous rice, pressed and warmed, with Mahachanok mango and a salted coconut cream that arrives in a tiny brass cup.

"To cook Thai food properly is to understand that every ingredient carries a conversation going back five hundred years. I am only the most recent voice."

Chef Lalana Sukhum · Head of Kitchen

Membership grants twelve monthly seatings, priority reservation, access to the private wine cellar, and invitations to off-season chef's table events. Applications are reviewed quarterly.

  • Private sommelier selections from our cellar — Thai natural wines, imported rarities
  • Quarterly off-menu tastings — new dishes before they ever appear
  • Access to the recipe archive: handwritten cards from three generations
  • One transferable guest invite per quarter
  • Invitation to the annual garden harvest and cook-together
