About Sala
Where the palace kitchens
meet the spirit of sharing
across a single table.

SALA was born from a conviction: that the most profound culinary experiences are intimate ones. Our chef spent years studying under Thailand's last royal recipe keepers before opening this eight-seat table in the heart of Bangkok. Every evening is a journey — not a performance.

Membership grants you access to a calendar of evenings, each one distinct, each one unrepeatable. The kitchen changes with the season, the market, the mood.

The intimacy
of eight.

We made a deliberate choice to stop at eight. Not twelve, not twenty — eight. Because at this scale, every guest is a presence. Conversation flows freely across the table. The chef can plate with intention, not urgency. A meal becomes an evening becomes a memory.

SALA accommodates dietary restrictions with advance notice. The menu is entirely chef-driven and changes with each new season. All produce is sourced within 200km of Bangkok, with exceptions made only for rare heritage ingredients from Thailand's north.

An evening at SALA

What to expect · Friday & Saturday · Seatings from 19:00

19:00

Arrival & Aperitif

Guests are welcomed into the salon with house-made Thai botanical spirits and cold towels. A quiet start before the table convenes.

I
19:30

The Table Sits

Eight guests gather. Introductions are made. The chef emerges to introduce the evening's theme and provenance of ingredients.

II
20:00

The Long Meal

Twelve courses arrive unhurried. Pairings are offered by the glass. The kitchen is open — questions are welcomed, interruptions are not.

III
23:00

The Lingering

After the last course there is no rush. Digestifs, conversation, and the quiet of a city pausing. Guests leave when they are ready.
