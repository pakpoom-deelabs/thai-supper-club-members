The Philosophy

A table reserved
for the curious.

Ngan — งาน, meaning "the work" — is the culmination of years spent in the markets of Chiang Mai, the royal kitchens of Bangkok, and the fishing villages of the South. Our chef, Khun Malee, interprets heritage recipes through a contemporary lens, without apology and without compromise.

Every fortnight, eight guests gather around a single table. The menu is never repeated. Ingredients arrive that morning. The evening ends when the conversation does.

What Awaits

Three pillars of the evening

01 —

The Ingredients

Single-source produce, heritage-breed proteins, and hand-pounded pastes. Nothing arrives in a packet. Everything has a story.

02 —

The Atmosphere

Hand-thrown ceramics, candlelight, and a curated selection of natural wines and Thai botanical cocktails to accompany each course.

03 —

The Craft

Khun Malee cooks, plates, and presents at the table. Guests witness the alchemy of each dish as it leaves the pass.
